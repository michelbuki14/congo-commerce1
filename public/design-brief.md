# CONGO COMMERCE — design brief

**Design read.** A continental commerce operating system for African sellers,
creators and enterprises, in a premium-technology register: confident,
infrastructural, fast, human. Audience: founders, sellers, creators and
enterprise buyers who judge a platform by whether it looks like it will still
exist in five years.

**Concept spine.** *The network that holds.* The site is one continuous descent
into a single commerce node that unfolds into a continental lattice. The film
enacts "one operating system for modern commerce" as a physical transformation:
one object becomes a connected network, then resolves. Every content section
after the journey continues the same idea in a different layout family.

**Delivery tier.** `spectacle` (cinema foundation plus generated icon set,
3D-adjacent node artwork, and a second signature beat in the content sections).

**Locked palette.** Dark, single accent, cool neutral base.

Defense: the Congo's actual material DNA is cobalt and copper country, so the
brand is built from a metal, not a stereotype. A deep jade signal on an
ink-green-black ground reads as premium African technology while dodging the
four generic looks the brief bans: it is not the banking blue, not the
shopping-site red-and-yellow, not the crypto neon-on-black, and not the gaming
acid palette. One accent, locked page-wide; copper appears only inside generated
imagery and graphic rules.

**Locked type.** Sora for display and UI (geometric, neo-grotesk, technology-led
per the brief's wordmark requirement). Manrope for body. JetBrains Mono for
numerals, prices, labels and data. No serif anywhere. Self-hosted.

Animation mode: animated-website

**Journey shape:** `single-shot`. The story is ONE subject, a commerce node,
seen ever more closely as it unfolds into a network, so a single continuous take
is the honest shape and leaves no seam to fake behind HTML copy.

**Journey** (5 chapters read over the one film):

1. `Start` — kicker none — **"Commerce, reimagined."** — Build, discover, sell,
   market, fulfill, and scale from one intelligent platform. Actions: *Start
   selling* (primary), *Explore the platform* (secondary). No tags.
2. `Marketplace` — **"Every product, one discovery engine."** — Listings ranked
   by intent, quality and delivery speed, not by who paid most. Tags: discovery,
   verified sellers.
3. `Sellers` — **"Run the business from a phone."** — Storefront, inventory,
   payments, payouts and staff permissions in one console that works on a
   low-end Android. Tags: mobile money, cash on delivery.
4. `Logistics` — **"From discovery to delivery."** — Couriers, pickup points and
   live tracking across every corridor, with delivery promises that hold.
   Tags: live tracking, pickup points.
5. `Intelligence` — **"One platform. Every commerce journey."** — AI
   merchandising, analytics and creator commerce running on shared
   infrastructure. Action: *Start selling* (primary).

**World grammar.** One byte-identical preamble: deep ink-green-black seamless
studio void; one hero object centred with clean negative space for chapter copy;
slow continuous forward drift with a gentle rise; jade emissive light
(`#12A87A`) as the only light colour; copper (`#C87A45`) metallic accents; ivory
(`#EDF2EE`) specular highlights; volumetric haze, shallow depth of field,
minimal anamorphic flare; locked exposure and white balance; minimal motion blur;
no cuts, no camera shake; opens on the lone node, resolves on the ignited
lattice. No on-screen text, logos or watermarks.

**Mobile framing.** Included by default. Mobile encodes cap at 720p height; the
focal object stays inside the centre-safe area; per-chapter `object-position`
keeps the node clear of the copy; chapter copy pins to the bottom safe area.

**Delivery budget.** ≤32 MiB across desktop clips, ≤16 MiB across mobile clips.

**Section plan** (home, after the journey; one layout family each, no repeats):
manifesto statement → five-cell platform bento → marketplace split (imagery) →
social commerce rail → seller console band → logistics full-bleed band → payments
ledger band → creator economy split → AI figures → 3D commerce band → plan cards
→ trust row → closing CTA band → footer.

**Asset plan.** Scroll-scrub film + its exact-frame posters; generated icon sheet
(sliced, background removed); logo family drawn as SVG in code (primary,
horizontal, stacked, icon, monogram); section imagery for marketplace, social
commerce, seller console, logistics, payments, creator economy, AI, 3D,
analytics; seamless node-grid pattern; OG card; favicon/head kit.

**CTA inventory** (each its own component, own interaction identity):
nav link with travelling arrow; hero primary oversized underlined link that draws
in and pulls magnetically; hero secondary label that splits open; plan-card
corner-bracket target that closes on hover; closing band that shears on hover;
footer mono sign-off that decodes on hover.
